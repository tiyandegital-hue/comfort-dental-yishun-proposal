Fees & Payment

Transparent fees, no surprises

We believe in honest pricing. All fees are discussed during consultation before any treatment begins — you’ll always know the cost upfront.

MediSave-Accredited Clinic

Comfort Dental Surgery is MediSave-accredited, which means eligible surgical dental procedures can be claimed under your MediSave account. This makes essential dental surgery more accessible and affordable.

How MediSave Works

  1. 1

    Consultation

    Visit us for an assessment — we’ll determine if your treatment is MediSave-eligible.

  2. 2

    Claim Assessment

    We’ll help you understand what can be claimed and the process involved.

  3. 3

    Treatment & Claim

    Receive treatment and we’ll guide you through the MediSave claim process.

Eligibility is confirmed during consultation, per MOH guidelines. We do not publish a procedure list — your eligibility depends on your specific case.
